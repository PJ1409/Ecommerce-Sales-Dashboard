# Executive Summary

## 1. Revenue Growth Is Strong but Losing Momentum

The ecommerce business generated over ₹150M in revenue during the analyzed period, with performance peaking in April at approximately ₹66.6M. However, revenue declined in the following months, dropping to ₹38.0M in May and further to ₹10.0M in June. While the business has demonstrated the ability to generate substantial revenue, the recent downward trend suggests that growth momentum may be slowing and requires closer monitoring.

The Revenue Growth Rate and Revenue Trend metrics indicate that management should investigate whether this decline is driven by seasonality, reduced demand, inventory constraints, or changes in customer behavior.

## 2. Revenue Is Concentrated in a Single Market

Country-level analysis reveals that India is by far the largest revenue contributor, generating approximately ₹112.7M in revenue. In comparison, the United States contributed around ₹20.8M, while transactions with missing country information accounted for approximately ₹16.8M.

This concentration suggests that the business currently relies heavily on one geographic market. While strong domestic performance is a positive indicator, expanding revenue contribution from additional countries could reduce market concentration risk and create more diversified growth opportunities.

## 3. A Small Group of Products Drives Business Performance

The Top 5 Products by Revenue analysis shows that a limited number of products account for a significant portion of total sales. Products such as **Marigold Home Craft Lite Wireless Earbuds**, **Everbloom Collective Air Over-Ear Headphones**, and several smartwatch products generate the highest revenue contribution.

This suggests that product performance is not evenly distributed across the catalog. Management should continue investing in high-performing products while evaluating whether underperforming products require pricing, promotion, or assortment adjustments.

Overall, the dashboard indicates a business with strong revenue generation capabilities, clear product leaders, and a dominant geographic market. The primary management focus going forward should be sustaining revenue growth, reducing dependence on a single market, and monitoring operational indicators such as refund rates to ensure long-term business health.
